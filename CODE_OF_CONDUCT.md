# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and focused on improving the project. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Serious or repeated issues may be reported through appropriate channels.

## Scope

This standard applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
